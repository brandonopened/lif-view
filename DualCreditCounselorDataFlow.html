Dual Credit Counselor Data Flow

As a college or high school counselor, I want a dashboard with aggregate data about dual credit students, and to drill from lists to individual, PII-integrated records of active dual credit students, so I can make resource decisions and advise students better.

The district stays on Ed-Fi and the college stays on its own LIF profile. What sits between them is the LIF data model, reached through the LIF Metadata Registry (MDR), with EDUcore supplying the schema knowledge and crosswalks the mapping is built from. Two figures: what happens once when the pair is set up, and what happens every time a counselor opens the dashboard.

4. The dual credit mapping, as built on the demo stack

Ed-Fi says "dual credit" on the enrollment, not the course: StudentSectionAssociation.DualCredit carries the indicator, a type descriptor (Grand Bend uses "Dual Enrollment"), a high-school-credit indicator, and the awarding institution as either an education organization reference or an institution descriptor. LIF has no dual-credit element. So the mapping does three things, each an ordinary registry operation:

  • Source side. The district's Ed-Fi model gains a StudentSectionAssociation entity with eleven attributes, created through the public MDR API by the alignment script, on both registries.
  • Target side. The college profile includes two base LIF attributes it did not expose before, Course.courseLevelCharacteristic and Course.recognizedBy, with one inclusion call each. No change to base LIF.
  • Rules. Two authored rules iterate the transcript courses and join to the student's sections inside the JSONata (LocalCourseCode = CourseCode and DualCreditIndicator = true), writing "Dual Credit" and the awarding organization onto the matching course entries and nothing onto the rest. They were drafted with the same CLI as the other rules and received as version 0.2 of each mapping.

Verified live: Grand Bend's two dual credit students, Jeremy Coleman and Diana Holt, translate through both colleges with ELA-01 flagged "Dual Credit", recognized by 9999 (descriptor path) and 6000203 (organization reference path). Students without a dual credit section are unchanged. The dashboard's cohort is then "persons with a course whose courseLevelCharacteristic is Dual Credit", and the aggregate is a count over those course entries by college and term.

What is real today and what is not. Real: the registry, translator, adapter contract, identity mapper, cache and GraphQL path exist and are the documented way to add a source; the Ed-Fi to college LIF mapping, dual credit included, runs live on the demo stack against Ed-Fi's Grand Bend sample data, row-safe, with every rule carrying its origin and a note. Not yet built: the Ed-Fi source adapter itself (the guide describes the contract; the demo posts to the translator directly), the dashboard, and the identity rows, which need a registration event or a shared state identifier. Two LIF behaviors to plan around: the Translator applies every mapping group registered for a source and target pair and does not pick a version, so superseded drafts must be deleted or deprecated; and DualCreditType and DualHighSchoolCreditIndicator have no target in this profile and are dropped, which the rule Notes state. EDUcore supplies one value crosswalk and the Ed-Fi element definitions today; property-level LIF anchors are the outstanding EDUcore work, and its connector token needs re-authorizing. PII in the drill-down is governed by the GraphQL API's credentials and the tenant scoping of the LIF deployment, not by anything in this flow.

5. Where LIF gets installed

Nothing is installed at the district. The district keeps its Ed-Fi ODS/API and grants one API client to the adapter. LIF runs at the college, or wherever the college's LIF tenant is hosted, and that is where the dashboard's GraphQL queries go. The repo's guide for this is docs/operations/guides/deploying-lif-at-your-institution.md.
